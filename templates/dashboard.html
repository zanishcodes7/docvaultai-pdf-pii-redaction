Data Engineering & Document Privacy

Redact sensitive fields before data moves downstream.

DocvaultAI turns raw, sensitive PDFs into privacy-safe documents and structured audit records. Built with Python, Flask, PyMuPDF, Docker, and Floci (local AWS S3 & SQS emulation), it detects and permanently masks emails, phone numbers, CNIC/IDs, payment cards/IBANs, and custom keywords—with automatic local fallback when Floci is offline.

01

What We Do

Automatically detect and permanently mask PII—emails, phones, national IDs/CNICs, card/IBAN numbers, and custom terms—inside multi-page PDFs.

02

What This Platform Is For

Keep unstructured documents safe before analytics, storage, or sharing, with side-by-side original vs. redacted previews, entity counts, and audit logs.

03

How The Pipeline Works

Connects with S3 buckets and SQS queues via Dockerized Floci on port 4566, and seamlessly falls back to local preview mode if Floci is stopped.
