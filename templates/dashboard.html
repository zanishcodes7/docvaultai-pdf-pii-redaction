Checking Floci
Automated PII Redaction & Cloud Pipeline

Sanitize sensitive PDFs before they enter downstream systems.

DocvaultAI is a data privacy and document engineering workflow built with Python, Flask, PyMuPDF, Docker, and local AWS S3/SQS emulation (Floci). It automatically finds and permanently masks sensitive entities—such as emails, phone numbers, CNIC/national IDs, payment cards/IBANs, and custom terms—while logging audit metadata and supporting both cloud-emulated and local fallback execution.

What We Do

Automated PDF PII Redaction

Unstructured PDFs like invoices, reports, applications, and records often contain sensitive personal or financial details. DocvaultAI scans PDF pages and permanently redacts matched emails, phone numbers, CNIC/IDs, card/IBAN numbers, and custom keywords.

Why This Website Exists

Safe, Verifiable Data Engineering

Instead of manually editing documents or guessing what got masked, this studio gives you a clear workflow: upload a document on one page, run and compare the original vs. redacted PDF on the next, and inspect entity counts, execution time, and audit logs.

Cloud & Local Resilience

S3, SQS & Local Fallback

When Dockerized Floci is running on port 4566, raw and redacted artifacts interact with S3 buckets and SQS queues. When Floci is offline, DocvaultAI automatically switches to local preview mode so you can still redact and export PDFs without interruption.
